# TIM Code of Conduct

TIM contributors must communicate professionally, review evidence rather than
people, and make room for disagreement about scientific and engineering
claims. Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory conduct, deliberate
misrepresentation of results, and disclosure of private or security-sensitive
information are not acceptable.

Review comments should identify the observed condition, its impact, and a
verifiable path to resolution. Contributors must disclose material conflicts
of interest and must not pressure reviewers to weaken an independent finding.

Concerns should be reported privately to the repository maintainer. The
maintainer may warn, restrict, or remove a participant and must preserve
confidentiality as far as reasonably possible. If the maintainer is involved,
the matter must be escalated to an independent reviewer selected by the
repository owner.

This policy applies to repository collaboration, issue and pull-request
discussion, project chat, reviews, and project events.
