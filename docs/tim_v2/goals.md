# TIM V2 Goals

## Primary goals

1. Preserve the frozen TIM Core and TIM-IM V1 behavior contracts.
2. Establish typed, versioned interfaces between all future modules.
3. Make datasets, claims, experiments, evaluations and decisions reproducible.
4. Provide deterministic provenance from input evidence to management output.
5. Isolate Research, Simulation, Paper, Shadow and Live execution domains.
6. Require scientific and risk gates before capability promotion.
7. Give agents read-scoped tools, budgets and explicit handoff contracts.
8. Maintain one Knowledge Layer instead of module-specific hidden truth stores.
9. Make failure, recovery and rollback paths observable and testable.
10. Support incremental delivery from V2.0 Foundation through V3.0 Research.

## Non-goals for the foundation

- no strategy or signal implementation;
- no live or shadow execution activation;
- no scientific model replacement;
- no historical result rewrite;
- no autonomous release or governance decision;
- no global external-agent configuration.
